\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

ACDB's instance family is fully observed, causally sufficient, linear--Gaussian, and restricted to hard single-node interventions; this is the regime in which CPDAG identifiability, the resolving-intervention set, and the closed-form random floor are exactly defined, and the reported ladder caps graph size at $d = 14$. The empirical study covers a five-model, five-policy panel evaluated through proprietary APIs at eight paired seeds per level, reports point estimates rather than confidence intervals, and evaluates prompt-only inference. Each of these axes --- non-linear and partially observed substrates, soft and multi-node interventions, larger graphs, denser panels, and fine-tuned or RL-trained agents --- is a natural extension that inherits ACDB's scoring discipline.
